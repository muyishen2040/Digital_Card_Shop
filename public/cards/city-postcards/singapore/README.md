# Singapore postcard artwork

- **Front:** Marina Bay Sands and Singapore Flyer at twilight.
- **Reverse:** Watercolor Peranakan shophouses, placed low on warm stationery to leave the middle clear for personalized HTML text.
- **Files:** `front.webp` and `back.webp` (3:4 portrait WebP assets).

These are stylized AI-generated city scenes, not verified documentary photographs. The reference-source and reproduction-rights record is not included here; confirm any external source or derivative rights before public distribution.
